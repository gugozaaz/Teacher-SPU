## 3. Core Formulas & Arithmetic
1. **Composite Venture Score (CVS) Formula (100 Points):**
   $$\text{CVS} = (0.20 \times \text{ASK}) + (0.15 \times \text{PREPARE}) + (0.25 \times \text{ANALYZE}) + (0.25 \times \text{ACT}) + (0.15 \times \text{STAGE})$$
   - GreenFresh Example: $(0.20 \times 95) + (0.15 \times 96) + (0.25 \times 96) + (0.25 \times 98) + (0.15 \times 94) = 19.0 + 14.4 + 24.0 + 24.5 + 14.1 = 96.0 / 100$ (Grade A+ Winner).
2. **Enterprise Data Project ROI (%) & Payback Period:**
   $$\text{Data Project ROI (\%)} = \frac{\text{Net Financial Payoff}}{\text{Total Project Cost}} \times 100$$
   $$\text{Payback Period (Months)} = \frac{\text{Total Project Cost}}{\text{Monthly Incremental Profit}}$$

## 4. Architectural Enhancements
- **10+5 Timekeeping Protocol:** 10 mins live pitch (BLUF -> Data -> Solution -> Payoff & ROI -> Roadmap) + 5 mins Q&A defense.
- **Stage Roles:** 4 C-Suite roles (CEO/Opener, CDO/Data Master, COO/AI Lead, CFO/Closer) with seamless handover cues.
- **Q&A Defense Tactics:** 3-second pause, Evidence-first shield, and Tag-team routing.
- **14-Week Retrospective:** 5-step lifecycle (ASK, COLLECT, PREPARE, ANALYZE, ACT) connected to the Data Flywheel.
- **Data Drift & SME Survival:** Handling shifting consumer habits with monthly audits and small-scale pilot tests.
- **Modern SME No-Code Data Stack:** Google Forms (Capture) -> Google Sheets (Clean & Store) -> Looker Studio (Dashboard) -> Gemini (AI Co-pilot) at 0 THB.
- **AI Master Prompts:**
  1. Venture Scorer & Rubric Auditor (Weighted CVS evaluation & feedback).
  2. 1-Year SME Data & AI Roadmap Planner (4 quarters foundation to automation).
